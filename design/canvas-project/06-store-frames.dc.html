Four claims this listing may not make

“Best”, “#1”, ticket prevention, and automatic detection are all ruled out on the record. The app does no motion or Bluetooth monitoring, so detection would be a false claim as well as a forbidden one.

The name is a draft, not a reservation

Collision checks on August 18, 2026 returned no exact App Store title match and 404 availability signals for the domains. Those are point-in-time signals — recheck immediately before creating the App Store record.

Frames one and two are the listing

Search results show two thumbnails. Frame one has to say what it does; frame two has to say why this one. Everything after frame two is for someone already interested.
