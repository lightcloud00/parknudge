ParkNudge · File 07

Three marks, one hand

Shown at true Home Screen scale, because that is the only size that matters and the size every icon concept is judged at too late. Each mark is an element already on a ParkNudge screen, not a metaphor for one.

HueH 205
GroundBone or ink
Built fromCSS geometry
Export1024px, no raster
StatusPre-release MVP

A — The saved pin

The exact ring-and-stem that marks the car on the Park screen. The most literal of the three and the only one that survives a 29px settings row unambiguously.

Recommended

B — The meter ring

A countdown caught at 72 percent. It says “timer” before it says “parking”, which is the right emphasis only if the meter feature leads the listing.

Alternate

C — The nudge

A mark above a ground line: the spot and the street it sits on. The most distinctive at thumbnail size and the least readable out of context.

Exploratory
1

A pale plate, not a saturated square

Almost every parking utility ships a filled blue or orange gradient tile. A bone plate with one accent mark is the cheapest differentiation in a results grid.

2

Both grounds are tested here

Row two is the same three marks on ink. Light plates win on a busy wallpaper; the ink versions hold up better in a dark-mode App Store listing.

3

No illustration, no raster

Every mark is a circle, a diamond, a bar and a conic gradient. There is nothing to redraw at 1024px and no illustrator in the loop.

4

The hue is the open question

These are drawn at H 205 while the shipping code still tints .orange. Whichever wins, the mark does not change — only the accent token does.
