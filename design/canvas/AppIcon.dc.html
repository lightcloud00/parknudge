App icon — three refinements
The shipped mark is already the right idea: an orange pin on the icon navy. Two things hold it back — a soft radial glow that reads as AI output, and a car grille whose two slots read as eyes at small sizes. Each concept keeps the pin and the palette and fixes one of those.
Shipping today

Same silhouette, glow removed, hard hue split top-to-bottom. Car keeps a real windscreen, so no face.
B · Pin & meter
Drops the car for the timer. One shape, one counter‑form — the most legible of the three at 29 pt.
C · Inverted
Navy pin on amber. Loudest in a home‑screen grid, furthest from what ships today.
The only test that matters
Home screen is 60 pt; Settings and Spotlight are 29 pt. Squint at the right‑hand column — that is the size most people will ever see this at.
