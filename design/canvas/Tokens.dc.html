Two oranges, on purpose, and the split is measured, not judged. brand is the fill that sits under white text — white on it is 3.21:1, over the 3:1 non‑text floor — and is never used for text. brandInk carries orange words: 5.57:1 on white, 4.86:1 on a grouped‑list fill, 11.2:1 on black. The app previously shipped one hardcoded .orange for both jobs.
brand has one value in both appearances. An earlier draft gave it a lighter dark variant (#FDAE11), which put the white label of the primary call to action at 1.87:1 in dark mode while reading fine in light. A light‑mode screenshot cannot show that — only measuring both schemes does.
