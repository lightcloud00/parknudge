ParkNudge privacy notice

ParkNudge keeps your parking records on your iPhone. There is no ParkNudge account, and Gus Digital Solutions runs no server that receives your parking data.

Data the app stores on your iPhone

ParkNudge stores parking coordinates, location accuracy, dates, labels, floor, section, notes, meter expiration, optional parking cost, reminder settings, and one compressed photo per session. This data stays in the app on your iPhone. ParkNudge has no account and no developer-operated backend.

Location, camera, and place search

ParkNudge asks for your location only when you tap Save Parking Spot, and only while you use the app. It never uses background location. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a parking photo. You can always place the pin by hand without location permission.

If you search for a garage, address, or landmark, the search text goes to Apple Maps to find places. Walking directions open Apple Maps with your saved spot.

Notifications and exports

Meter reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. With Lifetime Pro, you can export your parking history as a CSV file through the iOS share sheet. Exports leave out photos, and you decide where an exported file goes.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is an in-app purchase processed by Apple. ParkNudge never sees your payment details. On your iPhone, the app reads Apple's verified purchase record to unlock Lifetime Pro.

Tracking and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, remote push service, or third-party package, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

These web pages

If a ParkNudge web page offers a GDS email form, it accepts only the email address you enter for the message you request. It does not create a ParkNudge account or receive any parking data.

These pages use the GDS consent system. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity activate only after a visitor chooses “Accept.” Choosing “Decline” leaves both providers disabled. If accepted, they may receive browser, device, network, page, and interaction data. The website never receives parking coordinates, photos, history, exports, notification data, or app records.

Read the site-wide GDS privacy notice for provider details, retention, choices, and contact information.

Deleting your data

Deleting a session in History removes its photo and reminder requests. Settings › Delete All Parking Data removes all saved parking data from the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. There is no customer account or server-side ParkNudge record to delete.

Contact

Use the GDS contact form for privacy questions about ParkNudge. Please don't include parking coordinates, photos, exported history, receipts, or Apple ID details.
