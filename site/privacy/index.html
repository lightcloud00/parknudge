Privacy

ParkNudge is designed to keep your parking memory on your iPhone.

Data stored on device

Parking coordinates, capture accuracy, dates, labels, floor, section, notes, meter expiration, optional cost, reminder metadata, and one compressed photo per session are stored locally. ParkNudge has no account or backend.

Location and camera

Location is requested only after you tap Save Parking Spot. ParkNudge requests one fix and does not enable background location. Camera access is requested only when you choose Take Photo. You can use a manual map pin without location permission.

Notifications

Meter alerts are local notifications created from the expiration time you enter. ParkNudge does not use remote push notifications.

Exports

Lifetime Pro can create a CSV containing parking dates, duration, coordinates, accuracy, labels, notes, meter expiration, costs, and currency. Exports omit photo bytes and internal photo paths. The system share sheet controls where you send a file, and temporary export files are removed after sharing or at next launch.

Tracking and third parties

ParkNudge includes no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or third-party package. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple’s policies.

Deletion

Deleting a session removes its associated photo and notification requests. Delete All removes all app parking data from the device. Removing data is not reversible unless another system you chose to share it with retains a copy.

Contact

Support contact details will be finalized before public release. Until then, use the project’s GitHub issue tracker without posting sensitive location information.
