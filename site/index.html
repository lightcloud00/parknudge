Quick answers

Parking reminder questions

How do I remember where I parked?

Tap Save Parking Spot, check or move the map pin, add optional garage details, and save. When you are ready to return, open walking directions to the saved pin.

Can ParkNudge remind me before a parking meter expires?

Yes. Enter the expiration date and time. Free alerts use 15-minute, 5-minute, and expiry offsets when those moments are still in the future. Pro adds custom offsets and saved presets.

Does ParkNudge track me in the background?

No. Location is requested only after you tap Save Parking Spot and stops after one fix. There is no background location, account, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK.

What if location permission is denied?

You can search for a garage, address, or landmark and tap the map to place the pin manually. The complete free workflow remains usable.
