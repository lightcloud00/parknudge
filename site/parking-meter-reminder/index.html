Meter guide

Set a parking meter reminder from the real expiration time

An absolute date keeps a meter countdown honest even after the app closes and reopens.

Enter when the meter expires

Turn on Meter Timer while saving or editing a spot, then select the expiration date and time shown by the meter or parking app. ParkNudge calculates the display from that fixed time instead of decrementing a stored counter.

Free reminder schedule

The free tier schedules local warnings 15 minutes before, 5 minutes before, and at expiration. If you save an eight-minute meter, the 15-minute warning is already past and is omitted; the 5-minute and expiry alerts remain.

Custom reminders with Lifetime Pro

Pro lets you save up to five custom offsets. Extending or changing the meter cancels the old deterministic notification requests and replaces them with the new schedule.

Important limits

A reminder does not extend or pay a meter. Delivery depends on notification permission, Focus, device state, and iOS. Always check posted rules and the meter itself.

Privacy by default

Meter notifications are scheduled locally. There is no remote push server or background location tracking.
