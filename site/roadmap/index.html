Beta roadmap

What is built, and what still needs proof.

The roadmap distinguishes local implementation from physical-device, App Store commerce, deployment, and release acceptance.

MVP implementation

Core parking flow

Save one active spot, GPS and manual pin, place search, accuracy display, floor, section, note, photo, meter, Apple Maps directions, edit, replace, finish, history, and deletion.

Verified local architecture

SwiftData versioned schema, on-device photos, deterministic notifications, StoreKit 2 entitlement verification, cost minor units, RFC 4180 CSV, privacy manifest and local verification. Release acceptance also requires the matching native, device and purchase checks.

Website source ready

Eight indexable pages plus a real 404, social metadata, JSON-LD, canonical/sitemap parity, crawler files, Cloudflare path redirects, IndexNow verification, and a Google/Bing Engine handoff are checked in source.

Release and website checks

Public availability, website deployment, search indexing and purchase behavior require separate checks for the matching release.

Required before release

  • Reserve the public name and complete trademark review.
  • Acquire and configure the chosen domain, deploy the exact accepted commit, verify Google and Bing properties, and record sitemap and IndexNow receipts.
  • Run real-device GPS, camera, notification, Focus, and terminated-app checks.
  • Verify the existing Lifetime Pro product and test purchase, pending, cancellation, refund/revocation, restore and original-customer access in Apple Sandbox and on devices.
  • Complete accessibility, Dynamic Type, light/dark, Reduce Motion, and small/large iPhone review.
  • Prepare App Store metadata, screenshots, privacy answers, signing, TestFlight beta, and review submission.

After the MVP

  • Live Activity as a candidate Lifetime Pro feature.
  • Optional automatic parking suggestions only after privacy and battery validation.
  • Widgets, Apple Watch, CarPlay, iPad, and CloudKit remain deferred.

Revenue and responsible growth

  • Unlock parking for new customers with one Lifetime Pro purchase while preserving verified original customers’ parking access—no subscription or fake urgency.
  • Use aggregate App Store Connect and Search Console evidence instead of adding an analytics SDK.
  • Test product-page creative, intent-specific pages, localization, and useful search answers one variable at a time.
  • Keep paid acquisition behind an explicit budget, unit-economics, and stop-rule approval gate.
  • Refresh feature trends quarterly; Live Activity, automatic suggestions, Watch, and widgets remain evidence-gated candidates.

No release or revenue claim yet

This page describes work in the source repository. For current regional availability and purchase pricing, check the App Store. Source preparation alone does not establish device acceptance, deployment, indexing or revenue.
