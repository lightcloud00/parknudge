Parked-car guide

How to find your parked car with an iPhone

Save the location while you are still beside the car, add the detail GPS cannot see, and use walking directions when you return.

1. Save a precise parking pin

In ParkNudge, tap Save Parking Spot. The app requests one current-location fix, shows its accuracy, and stops location work. If the pin is approximate or misplaced, tap the map to correct it or search for the garage or address.

2. Record visual clues

In a multilevel garage, coordinates may not distinguish floors. Add the floor, section, a short landmark note, and one photo such as the nearest column marker. Avoid photographing people, license plates you do not need, or private information.

3. Walk back with Apple Maps

Open the active session and choose Walking Directions. ParkNudge passes the saved coordinate to Apple Maps. Garage floor and section notes remain visible in ParkNudge for the final indoor portion.

What if location permission is off?

You can complete the flow with place search and a manual map pin. ParkNudge does not require Always Location and does not track a trip in the background.
