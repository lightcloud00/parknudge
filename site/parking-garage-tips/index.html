Garage guide

How to remember where you parked in a garage

A garage spot needs more than latitude and longitude. Capture one detail from each layer of the route back.

Save floor and section separately

Record the numbered level and the colored or lettered zone in separate fields. They are easier to scan later than a long note.

Use a landmark note

Write a short cue such as “north elevator” or “across from stairwell B.” Prefer a fixed building feature over the color or model of a nearby vehicle.

Take one useful photo

Frame the column or aisle marker together with enough surroundings to recognize the approach. ParkNudge compresses and stores one photo locally with the session.

Correct the map pin before leaving

Garage structures can reduce GPS accuracy. Check the displayed accuracy and move the pin to the entrance or vehicle area you want walking directions to target.

Finish the session

When you leave, finish the active session so it moves to History. The free tier shows the three newest completed sessions while retaining older records locally.
