ParkNudge terms

ParkNudge is a parking memory aid for iPhone. It is not a parking enforcement, payment, navigation-safety, or emergency service.

Free parking and Lifetime Pro

Saving a parking spot, meter reminders, notes, a photo, and directions back are free, with no limit on parking sessions. Lifetime Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase tied to the Apple ID that buys it. There is no subscription and nothing renews. The App Store shows the price for your region before you confirm.

Lifetime Pro unlocks unlimited history, custom reminder times, parking cost records, and CSV export. Your saved parking data is never deleted when your access changes, and directions back to a saved spot and Finish always stay available.

Restoring and refunds

Use Restore Purchases in ParkNudge Settings or on the Lifetime Pro screen to restore a purchase made with your Apple ID. Apple processes payments and handles refund requests under its own policies through Apple's Report a Problem page.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for reading signs, following local rules, paying required fees, checking the actual meter, and parking safely and legally. ParkNudge cannot guarantee a space is legal or prevent tickets, towing, loss, or injury.

Reminder limits

Meter reminders depend on the date you enter, device settings, notification permission, Focus modes, battery state, and iOS delivery. A reminder may be delayed, skipped, or unavailable. Always check the meter directly.
