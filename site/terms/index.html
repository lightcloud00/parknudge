Terms

ParkNudge is a parking memory aid, not a parking enforcement or payment service.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for reading signs, following local rules, paying required fees, checking the actual meter, and parking safely and legally. ParkNudge does not guarantee that a space is legal or prevent tickets, towing, loss, or injury.

Reminder limits

Notifications depend on the date you enter, device settings, notification permission, Focus modes, battery state, and iOS delivery. A reminder may be delayed or unavailable.

Lifetime Pro

Lifetime Pro is planned as a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase tied to the purchasing Apple ID. Apple handles transactions, restoration, refunds, and revocation. Free parking features remain usable if the store is unavailable or a purchase fails.

Availability

The beta, website, price, feature set, and release schedule may change before public launch. These draft terms require final legal review before release.
